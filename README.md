# Flipkart-Sales-Analysis
Tools: Excel.  Cleaned and structured sales dataset for analysis.  Designed interactive dashboard to track sales trends and performance metrics.
Project Objective
To analyze the dataset and identify useful patterns, trends, and insights that support better business decisions.

Data Collection
The dataset was provided in Excel format, containing multiple fields such as product details, category, price, orders, dates, and customer info.

Data Cleaning
Removed null values, duplicates, formatting errors, and standardized categories to ensure accuracy in the analysis.

Data Transformation
Converted raw data into a structured format using filters, calculated columns, pivot tables, and charts for better readability.

Descriptive Analysis
Calculated key metrics such as total sales, average sales, number of orders, most-selling products, and monthly performance.

Trend Analysis
Identified seasonal or monthly variations in sales to understand high-performing and low-performing time periods.

Product Performance Insights
Highlighted top-selling and least-selling products/categories to focus on profitable items and improve inventory management.

Customer Behavior Analysis
Studied purchase preferences to determine which products customers buy the most and understand their spending patterns.

Visualization & Reporting
Used charts, graphs, and pivot dashboards to clearly present results and make insights easy for stakeholders to understand.

Business Recommendations
Suggested optimization strategies such as focusing on high-demand items, improving marketing during low-sales periods, and discount planning.
